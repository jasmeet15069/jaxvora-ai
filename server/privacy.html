Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 June 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Jaxvora ("Jaxvora", "we", "us") collects, uses, stores, and protects information when you use the Jaxvora autonomous multi-agent platform and its associated services (the "Service"). By using the Service you agree to the practices described here.

1. Information We Collect

  • Account & contact data — the email address you use to receive notifications and any profile details you provide.
  • Google account data (via OAuth) — if you connect a Google account, we access your Gmail data strictly within the scopes you authorize (reading, searching, drafting, sending, and organizing messages) to perform the email tasks you request.
  • Content you submit — chat messages, uploaded files, documents added to the knowledge base, résumé/profile fields for the job features, and instructions you give to the agents.
  • Operational data — task history, logs, and audit records needed to run, debug, and improve the Service.

2. How We Use Information

  • To provide the features you request (e.g. orchestrate AI agents, read/draft/send email, search and apply to jobs, parse files, answer questions over your knowledge base).
  • To send you notifications and results you have asked for.
  • To operate, secure, debug, and improve the Service.

Google API Services — Limited Use Disclosure

Jaxvora's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Specifically:

  • We only access your Google user data to provide and improve user-facing features you request.
  • We do not transfer or sell Google user data to third parties for advertising, marketing, or other unrelated purposes.
  • We do not use Google user data to train generalized/standalone AI or machine-learning models.
  • Humans do not read your Google user data unless you give explicit consent, it is necessary for security, to comply with law, or the data is aggregated and anonymized for internal operations.

3. AI / Third-Party Processing

To generate responses, Jaxvora sends the relevant content of your request to third-party large-language-model providers (such as Groq, OpenRouter, OpenCode Zen, and similar inference providers) solely to produce the output you asked for. These providers process the content under their own terms. We do not send your Google user data to these providers for model training, and we minimize what is shared to what is needed to fulfill your request.

4. Data Storage & Security

  • Sensitive personal data you provide for the job/application features (résumé PII) is encrypted at rest.
  • OAuth credentials and API secrets are stored only in the server runtime environment with restricted permissions — never exposed in the public application.
  • We apply reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your data. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

5. Data Retention & Deletion

We retain data only as long as needed to provide the Service or as required by law. You may request deletion of your stored content (chat history, uploaded files, knowledge-base documents, job profile) at any time. You can also revoke Jaxvora's access to your Google account at any time via your Google Account permissions page, which immediately stops all further access to your Gmail data.

6. Sharing

We do not sell your personal data. We share data only with the service providers needed to operate the Service (e.g. hosting and LLM inference providers), or when required by law.

7. Your Rights

Subject to applicable law, you may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data, and you may withdraw consent at any time. To exercise these rights, contact us using the details below.

8. Children

The Service is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age in your jurisdiction), and we do not knowingly collect their data.

9. Changes

We may update this Policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by updating the "Last updated" date above and, where appropriate, by additional notice.

10. Contact

Questions about this Privacy Policy or your data? Contact us at jaxvora@gmail.com.
